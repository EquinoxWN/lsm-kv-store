## Benchmarks

Command: `go test -run '^$' -bench . -benchmem -benchtime 3000x .` (100-byte values, 20-byte keys).

| Benchmark | Time per op | Throughput | Allocations |
|---|---|---|---|
| PutSync (fsync every write) | 2.34 ms | ~430 durable writes/s | 5 allocs, 351 B |
| PutNoSync | 8.4 µs | ~119,000 writes/s | 5 allocs, 351 B |
| Get (50,000 keys over memtable and L0 tables) | 15.5 µs | ~65,000 reads/s | 5 allocs, 5.7 KB |

### Reading the numbers

- Durable writes are bound by fsync latency on this disk. That is the cost of the durability
  guarantee, and it is the baseline for group commit in M3.
- Reads allocate 5.7 KB because each lookup reads a whole 4 KiB block without a block cache.
  L0 tables overlap, so a read may check several tables. Compaction (M2) and a block cache will
  change this, and these numbers are the "before".
- These are single-machine micro-benchmarks, not the YCSB comparison against sled planned for M3.
